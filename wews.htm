My Vow to You

My Love, My Ms. Beautiful, My Madam baby, My Sweetheart, My Darling, My Sweetipie, and My Honeybear ,

Dati, I really didn’t believe in greatest love. I thought it was just a childish thing. But then I found you. I never expected that a love this great could actually exist. Pinakilala mo sa akin ang tunay na kahulugan ng pag-ibig. Pinaramdam mo sa akin na I don’t need to question my worth anymore, kasi wala kang hinanap na kulang sa akin. Pero hindi mo alam, ikaw ang bumuo sa akin. You made me feel like I am worth choosing, and you made me see my own value.

I promise to cherish you every single moment of our lives. I can't wait to marry you in real life, to finally build the future we’ve been dreaming of. I promise to build a home with you. walang sigawan, walang sakitan, at walang tinatagong sikreto sa isa’t isa. Isang tahanan na puno ng kapayapaan, pagmamahal, at ingay ng mga pusa natin habang sabay tayong tumatanda. Gusto kong maging kakampi mo sa lahat. Gusto ko na ako ang unang tatakbuhan mo tuwing may problema ka.

I vow to support you in everything, to love you at your best or your worst, your highs or your lowest, your brightest days, or your darkest nights. Kakampi mo ako kapag pagod ka na, at ako ang magiging pahinga mo kapag masyado nang maingay ang mundo.

With all my love, forever yours — Gly
